# Sitting on a Fence Is More Than Just Sitting: Propagation in Timing Games on a Salop Circle

**Kazimierz Stańczak** · Independent researcher, Warsaw · Working paper, version of 10 November 2026

📄 [Read the paper (PDF)](Sitting%20on%20a%20fence%20in%20Salop%20Circle%20K_Stanczak%2011_10_2026%20v2.pdf)

## Abstract

Many firms use one irreversible action, such as a launch, exit or repricing date, in several markets. I study timing games on a Salop circle where each market has a central firm and two edge firms; each edge firm competes in two neighboring markets with one date. In mixed-strategy equilibrium, a firm's payoffs determine its rivals' timing. Conditional on two equilibria sharing a mixing interval adjacent to the pinned endpoint, a local change travels around the circle: unchanged firms pass it on in full, with alternating sign. With equal exposure, the response is uniquely pinned on odd circles, whereas even circles cannot accommodate a nonzero local change on such an interval. The signs of the individual timing responses reverse between preemption and attrition.
