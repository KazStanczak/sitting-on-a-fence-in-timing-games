# Sitting on a Fence Is More Than Just Sitting: Propagation in Timing Games on a Salop Circle

**Kazimierz Stańczak** · Independent researcher, Warsaw · Working paper, version of 19 November 2026

📄 [Read the paper (PDF)](Sitting%20on%20a%20fence%20in%20Salop%20Circle%20K_Stanczak%2011_10_2026%20v2.pdf)

## Abstract

Firms often use one launch, exit or repricing date in several markets. I study precommitted timing games on a Salop circle: each market has a central firm and two edge firms; each edge firm uses one date in two neighboring markets. In mixed-strategy equilibrium, a firm’s payoffs pin its rivals’ timing. On a mixing interval shared by two equilibria at the pinned endpoint, a local change travels around the circle: unchanged firms pass it on in full, with alternating sign. With equal exposure, the response is unique on odd circles, impossible on even ones. Signs reverse between preemption and attrition.
